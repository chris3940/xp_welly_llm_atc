* --- LYON TMA SECTOR 2.2 (missing from the Navigraph openair export) -----------
* The vendor export ships LYON TMA SECTOR 1, 2.1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 6.1, 7, 8, 10, 11 --
* but NOT 2.2 (nor 9). SECTOR 2.2 (class E, 3500 ft - FL065) covers the Rhone
* valley / Valence departure area SOUTH of the other Lyon TMA sectors (they start
* ~45:16N). Without it, openair was empty at ~45:02N / 5300 ft on a LFLU departure
* and the enroute resolver fell back to the atc.dat nearest ACC -> MARSEILLE (wrong;
* the aircraft is still with LYON). Vertices user-read from the published chart
* (2026-08-03). "AF" pins the controller frequency to Lyon Approach 125.155 -- the
* same freq the IFR clearance was delivered on, so entering this sector is a SILENT
* handoff (already on freq) and the aircraft simply stays with Lyon. atc.dat's
* generic LYON tracon is 123.70/131.315, which would instead move the pilot off the
* clearance freq. [C. P. Potter]
