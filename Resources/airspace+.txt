* Custom Data/airspaces/airspace.txt; overlay wins on same name. Cross-border
* delegation polygons are matched by NAME: "DELEGATED BY <from> TO <ICAO>" ->
* engine resolves the delegated ACC (ICAO) and takes its atc.dat frequency.
* Two same-name entries coexist (the loader appends all overlay entries).

* handed off to Ljubljana there (Milan -> Glockner direct). Published chart gives
* two bands for the SAME polygons: FL145-FL195 and FL195-FL660 -> one AC C volume

* D = the lower bevel). Together with the ACC visited-guard the aircraft stays on
* Ljubljana across the small gap between them. [C. P. Potter]
